Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Pinr.

Getting started

What is Pinr?

Pinr is a travel app built around a globe. You drop a pin for a place you have been, using a photo, and your friends see it on their own globe. You can also join pins into Journeys, keep a Bucket List, collect badges in your Passport and play geography games against friends.

How do I get around the app?

The bar along the bottom has five places:

  • Globe: your pins and your friends' pins. The search key at the top finds places.
  • Friends: your friends, friend requests and news from them.
  • Drop pin: the pin-shaped key in the middle. This is how you drop a pin.
  • Play: the games, challenges, your streak and the leaderboard.
  • Passport: your badges, pins, Journeys and Bucket List. The gear at the top opens Settings.
Is Pinr free to use?

Yes, Pinr is free to download and use.

What phones does Pinr work on?

Pinr is available for iPhone and Android. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play.

Pins

What is a pin?

A pin marks a place you have been. It has a photo, a place, a date and, if you like, a title. It appears on your globe showing your profile photo (or your initial if you have no photo), ringed in your colour, so friends can tell at a glance whose it is.

How do I drop a pin?
  1. Tap Drop pin, the key in the middle of the bar at the bottom.
  2. Choose photos from your photo library. You can pick up to 10.
  3. Pinr fills in the place and the date from the photo and marks them from photo. Tap either one to change it.
  4. Add a title if you want one, and check Who sees it and Stays for.
  5. Tap Drop pin.

You will see "Pinned" and the place name, with an Undo button for a few seconds.

My photo has no place or date. What do I do?

Some photos do not carry a place, for example if location was off when you took them. Tap the place and search for it. The pin needs a place before you can drop it.

If the photo has no date, Pinr starts with today. Tap the date to pick the right day, or just the month.

What happens if I choose more than one photo?

One photo makes a pin. Two or more make a Journey, with one stop for each photo. See "What is a Journey?" below.

How long does a pin stay on the globe?

You choose with Stays for: 1 day, 1 week or Forever. Forever is the default. The pin shows how long is left, such as "6 d left". When the time is up the pin leaves the globe and is removed. You can change this later by editing the pin.

Can I edit a pin?

Yes. Tap your pin on the globe, then Edit. You can change the title, the place, the date, how long it stays, and add photos or bring a different photo to the front. Tap Save when you are done.

Can I delete a pin?

Yes. Open your pin, tap Edit, then Delete pin. Pinr asks "Delete this pin?" and tells you it leaves your globe and your friends' globes. Straight after deleting you get an Undo button for a few seconds. After that it is gone.

Can I hide a pin?

You can hide a friend's pin from your own globe. Open it, tap the ... key, then Hide this pin. The pin is not deleted for anyone else.

To hide everything from one friend, open their profile from the Friends place, tap the ... key and switch on the option that hides their pins on your globe. Switch it off to see them again.

To stop someone seeing your own pins, use Privacy (see "Who can see my pins and Journeys?").

Journeys

What is a Journey?

A Journey joins your pins into a trip that plays on the globe, stop by stop. Friends can get a notification when you post one.

How do I make a Journey?
  1. Tap Drop pin and choose two or more photos.
  2. Check the place and date of each photo, then tap Next.
  3. Put the stops in the order you want and give the Journey a name.
  4. Check Who sees it and Stays for, then tap Post journey.
Can I change or delete a Journey?

Yes. Open your Passport, go to the Journeys tab and open the menu on the Journey. Choose Edit journey to rename it, change the cover or add one of your pins. At the bottom is Delete journey. Deleting a Journey also deletes its pins, and it cannot be undone.

Bucket List

What is the Bucket List?

The Bucket List is your list of places to go. Each place is marked Wishlist, Booked or Visited, and you can add a date. It is a list, not pins on the globe. You will find it in your Passport on the Bucket List tab.

How do I add a place to my Bucket List?

There are two ways:

  • Search for a place with the search key at the top of the Globe, then tap Add to Bucket List.
  • Open a friend's pin and tap Add to Bucket List.

Open a place in your Bucket List to change its status or date, to Drop a pin here once you have been, or to Remove from Bucket List.

Friends

How do I add friends?

There are two ways:

  1. Find by username: open Friends, tap Find by username and type at least two letters of their username.
  2. Send your link: open Friends and tap your invite link, then send it by Messages, WhatsApp or Mail, or copy it. The link works on iPhone and Android. When your friend opens it they see "invited you" and can tap Add friend. If they do not have Pinr yet, the link takes them to the App Store or Google Play.

Either way, this sends a friend request. It is not a friendship until they accept.

How do friend requests work?

Requests you receive are in the Activity tab of Friends. Tap Accept, or the round key to decline. You also get a notification when someone asks. Once you are friends you see each other's pins on your globes and can challenge each other to games.

I cannot find my friend by username

Check the spelling, and type at least two letters. If it still does not work, send them your invite link instead.

Can I remove a friend?

Yes. Open the friend's profile from the Friends place, tap the ... key and choose Remove friend. You will stop seeing each other's pins. You can add each other again later.

Can I hide a friend's pins without removing them?

Yes. Open their profile, tap the ... key and switch on the option that hides their pins on your globe. You stay friends. Switch it off whenever you like.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile, tap the ... key and choose Block. They will not see your pins, find you or challenge you, and you will not see theirs. If you were friends, the friendship ends.

To see or undo your blocks, open your Passport, tap the gear, then Privacy and Blocked people. Tap Unblock next to a name.

Games

What games are in Pinr?

There are four. Each has Easy, Medium and Hard.

  • Pin Drop: five rounds. Find the place on the globe, then tap Drop pin. The closer you are, the more points you score, and time left adds a bonus. Rounds last 30, 20 or 15 seconds depending on the level.
  • Travel Battle: multiple choice, with Flags or Trivia. You have 30 seconds. Each right answer scores 10 points, plus 2 more for every right answer in a row.
  • Scramble: unscramble the name of a place against a 60-second clock. Each name has a clue, such as "City in Portugal". A letter scores 10 points. A hint places the next letter, costs 3 seconds and halves the points for that name.
  • Line Up: put four places in order, such as west to east or smallest to largest, then tap Check. You have 60 seconds and can do as many sets as you can. Each place in the right position scores 10, so a perfect set is 40.
How do I play?

Open Play, choose a game, choose Easy, Medium or Hard and tap Play. The first time you open a game, Pinr shows you how it works. If you leave a game part-way, your score does not count.

How do I challenge a friend?
  1. Open Play and choose a game and a level.
  2. Tap Challenge a friend and tap Send next to a friend.
  3. You play straight away. Your friend gets a notification and has 24 hours to play.

The higher score wins, and a tie is possible. Your challenges are listed in three groups: Your turn, Waiting and Finished. If a challenge is waiting for you, tap Play, or Decline if you would rather not. Challenges work in Pin Drop and Travel Battle at the moment.

How do streaks work?

Your streak counts the days in a row that you finish a game. Winning is not needed, finishing is. One missed day each week is covered, using your own calendar day, so a single slip does not end a long streak. After a longer break, Pinr shows your best streak and you start again from one.

How does the leaderboard work?

Open Play and tap Leaderboard. The boards are All games, Pin Drop, Travel Battle, Scramble and Line Up. A single game's board ranks you and your friends by best score. All games ranks by friends beaten: you get one point for every friend you are ahead of in each game, added together. Only you and your friends are on the boards.

Passport

What is the Passport?

Your Passport is your travel profile. The top shows how many continents and countries you have been to. It has four tabs: Badges (one for each country), Pins, Journeys and Bucket List. Countries come from the places of your pins, plus any Bucket List place marked Visited. You can open a friend's Passport too, from the ... key on their pin.

How do I change my username, photo or colour?

Open your Passport, tap the gear, then Edit profile. You can:

  • change your profile photo, or show your initial instead
  • change your username (3 to 20 characters; it has to be free)
  • write a bio of up to 80 characters
  • pick your colour from six: Ember, Cobalt, Magenta, Teal, Saffron or Violet

Your profile photo, or initial, is the face on every pin you drop, and your colour is how friends spot you on their globe.

Privacy and safety

Who can see my pins and Journeys?

Your friends. People who are not your friends cannot see them. You can narrow it down in Settings: open your Passport, tap the gear, then Privacy.

  • Friends: all your friends see your pins.
  • Only me: none of your friends do.
  • Choose: you switch friends on or off one by one.

People you block cannot see your pins either.

How do I report a pin or a person?

To report a pin, open it, tap the ... key and choose Report. To report a person, open their profile, tap the ... key and choose Report. Pick a reason:

  • Inappropriate photo or text
  • Spam
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Something else

You can add details if you want, and switch on Also block to block them at the same time.

What happens after I report something?

Every report is sent to us to review. A reported pin leaves your globe straight away. In most cases it is also hidden from everyone except its owner until we have looked at it. We then either put it back or delete it. Reports about a person are reviewed by us too.

Are photos and titles checked?

Yes. When you post, Pinr checks your photo and any title. If one is stopped, Pinr says why. Photos must be your own and safe for everyone, and titles must be kind. You can read the rules in Settings under Help and legal, then Terms and Code of Conduct.

What does "Account paused" mean?

In serious cases we can pause an account. Pinr then shows an Account paused screen that says why and has an Ask for a review button.

Is my data private and secure?

We take your privacy seriously. Your pins are shown to your friends only, and you choose which friends in Privacy. For more detail, please read our Privacy Policy.

Your account

How do I sign in?

On the first screen, choose one of three:

  • Continue with Apple
  • Continue with Google
  • Continue with email: sign in or create an account with your email address and a password

You stay signed in on your phone until you sign out.

I forgot my password

Choose Continue with email, tap Forgot password?, enter your email address and tap Send reset link. If you sign in with Apple or Google you do not have a Pinr password.

How do I change my password or sign out?

Open your Passport, tap the gear, then Account. There you can see how you sign in, Change password (not for Apple or Google sign-in) and Sign out.

How do I delete my account?

Open your Passport, tap the gear, then Account and Delete your account. Pinr shows you what will go, then asks you to confirm it is you. If you signed in with Apple or Google, you confirm with Apple or Google. With email, you type your password and tap Delete my account.

Deleting removes your pins and their photos, your Journeys, your Bucket List, your friendships and your game scores, along with your profile and profile photo. It cannot be undone.

How do notifications work?

Pinr sends a notification when:

  • a friend drops a pin
  • a friend posts a Journey
  • someone sends you a friend request
  • a friend challenges you to a game
  • a challenge is decided

To choose which ones you get, open your Passport, tap the gear, then Notifications. There is one switch for all of them, and one each for Friends' pins, Friends' journeys, Game challenges and Game results.

If something goes wrong

I am not getting notifications

First check Settings, then Notifications, in Pinr. The main switch needs to be on, and so does the kind you are waiting for. Turning the main switch on asks your phone for permission again. If you still get nothing, check that notifications are allowed for Pinr in your phone's own Settings.

My pin did not save

If a pin does not save, Pinr tells you "The pin did not save" and keeps your photos on the screen. Check your connection and tap Drop pin again.

A photo cannot be posted

Pinr checks each photo and title when you post. If the check stops one, you will see "This photo can't be posted" or "This caption can't be posted". Choose another photo or edit the title. Photos must be your own and safe for everyone.

What if I have no connection?

Pinr shows "No connection. Showing your saved globe." and gives you what it already has. When your connection returns it says "Back online".

I see "Something went wrong"

Tap Try again. If it keeps happening, tap Tell us what happened or write to us at lee@getpinr.com.
